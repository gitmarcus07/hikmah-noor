The fourth Kalima, *Tawheed* (Oneness), unfolds the short testimony into a full portrait of Allah.

## Fourth Kalima in Arabic

> لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ يُحْيِي وَيُمِيتُ وَهُوَ حَيٌّ لَا يَمُوتُ أَبَدًا أَبَدًا ذُو الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ بِيَدِهِ الْخَيْرُ وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ

## What each part affirms

- **Wahdahu la sharika lah** — alone, without partner in worship, lordship or names.
- **Lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamd** — all ownership and all praise belong to Him.
- **Yuhyi wa yumit** — He alone gives life and death.
- **Hayyun la yamut** — the Living who never dies, unlike every creature.
- **Dhul-jalali wal-ikram** — Lord of Majesty and Honour (a divine name to plead by).
- **Bi yadihil-khair** — all good is in His hand.

Learn it after the [third kalima](/kalimas/third-kalimah-tamjeed/) and continue to the [fifth kalima](/kalimas/fifth-kalimah-astaghfar/).
