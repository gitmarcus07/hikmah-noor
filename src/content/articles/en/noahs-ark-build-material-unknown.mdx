**Short answer: the method is known, the species is unknown.** The Quran says Nuh's Ark was a wooden vessel of planks joined with fasteners — and names no wood type. Viral claims that "scientists say the material is unknown" come from the Durupinar drilling in Turkey, where no timber has been recovered at all.

If you are following the discovery claim, read [Noah's Ark Found in Turkey? Durupinar 2026 Drilling](/waqiat/noahs-ark-turkey-durupinar-fact-check/) first, then the Quranic story in [Nuh — The Ark and the Flood](/waqiat/nuh-ark-flood/) and [Nuh — Nine Centuries and the Ark](/prophets/nuh-nine-centuries-ark/).

## Why "material unknown" is trending

Short videos claim the boat-shaped mound in Turkey tested as "unknown material" — proof, they say, of a miraculous ship. What actually happened:

* **June 29 – Sept 11, 2026:** mapping, ground-penetrating radar and soil sampling at Durupinar near Dogubayazit, about 29 km south of Mount Ararat.
* **~1,000 soil samples** showed higher carbon/organic readings inside the formation than outside, plus undated pottery fragments nearby.
* **September 2026:** permitted core drilling to 18 metres began; the team reported a hard layer at 4–5 metres.
* **Cores sent to Ankara** — results expected winter 2026–2027.

"Unknown material" here means **no identified timber exists**. Radar shapes and carbon-rich soil are not a wood sample. The team itself has not concluded it is the Ark.

## What the Quran actually says it was made of

> `And We carried him on a construction of planks and fasteners (dusur).` — Quran 54:13

Two verses frame the whole build:

1. **Built under divine watch:** `build the ark under Our watch and revelation — and do not plead for the wrongdoers` (11:37, also 23:27). Nuh built on dry land while chiefs mocked him (11:38–39).
2. **Built of wood, plank-joined:** `dhat alwah wa dusur` — planks (`alwah`) held by `dusur`: pegs, dowels, nails or lashings, according to classical tafsir (Ibn Kathir, Jalalayn tradition). A wooden ship, human-built, divinely guided.
3. **Landed by mercy:** `it settled on Judi` (11:44) — the Quran names Judi, not Ararat specifically.

That is the complete revealed specification. The Quran gives no measurements, no wood species, no nail count.

## What Islam does not claim

* **Not "gopher wood."** That word is from Genesis 6:14. It has no basis in the Quran or authentic hadith — do not present it as the Islamic position.
* **Not teak as fact.** Some history books mention `saj` (teak), but these are weak, unsourced reports. Scholars do not treat them as revelation.
* **Not proof from Durupinar.** Turkey's `Nuhun Gemisi` road sign and national-park name are tourism labels, not evidence. Affirm the Ark from revelation; wait for lab evidence on any mound.

## Why scientists say the material is still unknown

* 1960 expedition dug and dynamited inside the formation for two days: only soil and rocks, `no visible archaeological remains`.
* 1996 peer-reviewed paper (Collins & Fasold): natural `doubly plunging syncline` — limonite/magnetite layers mistaken for walls, metamorphosed peridotite mistaken for bark.
* Even creationist geologists who once hoped (1987–88 surveys and drilling) concluded it is natural.
* Radar shows shapes; only drilled, lab-tested, dated material counts — and none has been produced.

Until Ankara publishes, the honest headline is: **interesting site under test, material unidentified because nothing man-made has been recovered.**

## What to watch next

* Ankara lab: decayed wood or just carbon-rich soil?
* Radiocarbon dating — only if suitable organic material exists.
* Independent review, not team press releases alone.
* 2027 publication: `Durupinar Formation 2026 Research`.
