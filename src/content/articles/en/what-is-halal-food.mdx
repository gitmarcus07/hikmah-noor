*Halal* means permissible. For food, it covers ingredients, sourcing, preparation and handling.

## Halal vs Haram basics

**Haram includes:** pork and products, alcohol/intoxicants, blood, carrion, meat not slaughtered Islamically, food mixed with these.

**Halal includes:** fruits, vegetables, grains, milk, eggs, fish/seafood (by majority view), and meat from *zabiha* — slaughter mentioning Allah's name, cutting throat swiftly, draining blood.

## Zabiha in brief

Slaughter by a sane Muslim/Person of Book, saying *Bismillah Allahu Akbar*, minimal suffering, full bleed. Many Muslims look for halal certification for packaged meat.

## Practical tips

- Read labels for gelatin, lard, whey, wine vinegar.
- Ask restaurants about oil, broth, cross-contact.
- When unsure while traveling, choose vegetarian/fish and say Bismillah.

Halal is both physical purity and God-consciousness (*taqwa*) in eating.
