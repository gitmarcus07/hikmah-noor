The third Kalima, *Tamjeed* (glorification), gathers the four greatest phrases — plus the treasure of Paradise.

## Third Kalima in Arabic

> سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ وَلَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَاللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ الْعَلِيِّ الْعَظِيمِ

## Phrase by phrase

- **SubhanAllah** — Allah is free of every defect ascribed to Him.
- **Alhamdulillah** — all praise, gratitude and perfection belong to Allah.
- **La ilaha illallah** — the testimony itself, heaviest on the scales.
- **Allahu Akbar** — Allah is greater than every worry, enemy and desire.
- **La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah** — no power to obey or resist except through Allah.

## Why it matters

The Prophet ﷺ said these phrases are **the most beloved words to Allah** (Muslim), and that each *SubhanAllah* or *Alhamdulillah* fills the scales or what is between heaven and earth. The third kalima puts them in one string for daily dhikr.
