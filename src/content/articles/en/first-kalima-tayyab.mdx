The first Kalima, *Tayyab* (pure word), is the entry to Islam and the core of faith.

## First Kalima in Arabic

> لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ مُحَمَّدٌ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ

## Transliteration

La ilaha illallah, Muhammadur Rasulullah.

## Meaning

None is worthy of worship except Allah; Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah.

## Why it matters

- **Negation + affirmation:** no false gods, only Allah.
- **Two parts:** *Tawheed* (Oneness of Allah) and *Risalah* (Prophethood of Muhammad).
- Recited with belief, understanding and action — prayer, honesty, mercy.

Teach children early, repeat often, live by its meaning.
