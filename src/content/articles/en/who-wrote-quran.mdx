A common question: who wrote the Quran?

## Islamic answer

Muslims believe the Quran is **revelation (*wahy*) from Allah**, not the Prophet's composition. Angel Jibril brought verses over 23 years; the Prophet recited, companions memorized and scribes wrote on parchment, bones and leaves.

The Prophet himself was unlettered (*ummi*), which Muslims see as proof the eloquent text is divine.

## Human role: preservation

- **During life:** dozens of scribes (e.g. Zaid ibn Thabit), plus mass memorization.
- **After death:** under Caliph Abu Bakr, scattered sheets gathered into one manuscript after Yamama.
- **Under Uthman:** copies made and sent to regions to prevent dialect disputes — the *Uthmani mushaf* used worldwide today.

## Why Muslims are confident

Continuous mass transmission (*tawatur*), early manuscripts (e.g. Birmingham, Topkapi), and living memorization by millions in every generation.
