*Tawakkul* means placing trust in Allah while taking the means.

## Quranic basis

> وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى اللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسْبُهُ — Whoever trusts in Allah, He is enough for him (65:3).

## Tying the camel

A man asked: leave camel free and trust? The Prophet said: **tie it, then trust**. Students study, patients take medicine, travelers plan — then hearts rest with Allah's decree.

## How to practice

1. Do your best with halal means.
2. Make dua for outcome.
3. Accept result without anxiety or envy.
4. Thank Allah in success, be patient in delay.

Tawakkul removes fear of people and future, bringing calm and courage.
