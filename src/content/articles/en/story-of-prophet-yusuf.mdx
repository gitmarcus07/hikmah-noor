Surah Yusuf is called *ahsan al-qasas* — the best of stories.

## Dream and jealousy

Young Yusuf dreams eleven stars, sun and moon prostrating. His father Yaqub warns not to tell jealous brothers. They throw him in a well; travelers take him to Egypt as a slave.

## Trial and prison

Raised in Aziz's house, Yusuf resists temptation, chooses prison over sin. In prison he gives dawah and interprets dreams truthfully.

## From prison to power

His interpretation of the king's dream (seven fat/thin cows) saves Egypt from famine. He becomes treasurer, meets brothers who once wronged him, tests them, then forgives: *No blame on you today*.

## Reunion

Family reunites, dream fulfilled. Lessons: guard chastity, be patient in well and prison, forgive when powerful, trust Allah's plan even when others plot.
