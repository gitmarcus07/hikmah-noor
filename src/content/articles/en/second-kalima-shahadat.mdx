The second Kalima, *Shahadat* (testimony), turns belief into witness.

## Second Kalima in Arabic

> أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ

## Transliteration

Ashhadu an la ilaha illallah, wahdahu la sharika lah, wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan abduhu wa rasuluh.

## Meaning

I bear witness that none is worthy of worship but Allah — alone, without partner — and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and His Messenger.

## Why both "servant" and "Messenger"?

- **Abduhu (His servant):** blocks exaggeration — no matter his rank, Muhammad worshipped Allah and is never worshipped.
- **Rasuluhu (His Messenger):** blocks denial — his commands and Sunnah are binding guidance, not optional advice.
- This balance defines Sunni belief: love him deeply, follow him fully, worship Allah alone.

## Where you recite it

- **Tashahhud** in every salah — the wording the Prophet taught Ibn Masud.
- **After wudu** — whoever completes it opens the eight gates of Paradise (Muslim).
- **Embracing Islam** and in the **adhan** five times daily.
