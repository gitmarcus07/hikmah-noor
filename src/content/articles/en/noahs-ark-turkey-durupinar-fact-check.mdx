**Short answer: No — Noah's Ark has not been found.** Researchers are drilling at an interesting site in Turkey, but viral videos saying "found" go far beyond the evidence.

If you want the Quranic story first, read [Nuh — The Ark and the Flood](/waqiat/nuh-ark-flood/) and [Nuh — Nine Centuries and the Ark](/prophets/nuh-nine-centuries-ark/).

## What is going viral?

Short videos claim a boat-shaped mountain in Turkey is the Ark, with radar images showing walls and rooms. Some say scientists proved it in 2026. That last part is not true.

What is true: a permitted scientific season ran in summer 2026, and drilling has just begun. Findings so far are intriguing but preliminary.

## Where is this site?

The **Durupinar formation** near Dogubayazit, Agri Province, eastern Turkiye:

* About 29 km south of Mount Ararat, Turkey's tallest peak
* About 157 metres (515 feet) long, boat-shaped when seen from above
* Exposed by rains and earthquakes in 1948, noted from the air in 1959 by Captain Ilhan Durupinar
* Turkey has a road sign `Nuhun Gemisi` (Noah's Ship) and calls the area a national park — a tourism name, not a scientific proof

Its length is close to the biblical description of 300 cubits, which is why it attracts attention. The Quran does not give measurements for the Ark.

## What really happened in 2026?

Led by Prof. Cenker Atila of Sivas Cumhuriyet University with the US-based Noah's Ark Scans team (Andrew Jones) and about 20 researchers from five countries:

* **June 29 – Sept 11, 2026:** mapping, ground-penetrating radar and BakhtarRadar scans, GPS, 2D/3D models
* **~1,000 soil samples** from 150 points; carbon/organic readings higher inside the formation than outside
* **Pottery fragments** found nearby — age not yet dated
* **Radar anomalies:** angular features, voids, and layering up to several metres deep, described as possible tunnels, corridors, or three layers like decks
* **September 2026:** permitted core drilling to 18 metres started; team reported the bit hit a hard layer at 4–5 metres
* **Cores sent to Ankara** for lab work on organic material and possible wood; results expected winter 2026–2027, with a book and three papers planned for 2027

The team itself has **not concluded** it is the Ark. Their stated aim is to test whether it is natural or contains man-made remains.

## Islamic view: hold to Judi, hold back from claims

> `And it was said: O earth, swallow your water, and O sky, withhold. And the water subsided and the matter was decided, and it settled on Judi.` — Quran 11:44

Muslims believe Nuh (peace be upon him) called his people for `a thousand years less fifty` (29:14), built the Ark under Allah's watch while mocked (11:37-39), loaded pairs and believers (11:40), and landed by Allah's mercy (11:48). See Surah Nuh (71) in full.

Two cautions from that same Quran:

1. The Quran names **Judi**, not Ararat specifically. Classical scholars differ on Judi's location. We do not need to force Durupinar to fit.
2. Even a prophet was corrected about his own son: `he is not of your family — his deed was unrighteous` (11:46). Evidence matters more than wishes.

So: affirm the Ark as revelation, wait for evidence on any modern mound. Allah knows best.

## Why scientists remain skeptical

* 1960 expedition dug and dynamited inside the formation for two days: only soil and rocks, `no visible archaeological remains`
* 1996 peer-reviewed paper by Lorence Collins and David Fasold: natural `doubly plunging syncline`, limonite/magnetite layers mistaken for walls, metamorphosed peridotite mistaken for bark, anchor stones near Kazan are local andesite, not from Mesopotamia
* Even creationist geologists who once hoped — including Andrew Snelling, John Morris, John Baumgardner after 1987-88 surveys and drilling — concluded it is natural
* No wood, petrified timber, nails, or metal has ever been extracted; 2026 claims are press statements, not peer-reviewed papers yet

Radar can show shapes, but shapes alone do not prove a ship. Only drilled material, lab-tested and dated, can move this forward.

## What to watch next

* Ankara lab: is there decayed wood or just carbon-rich soil?
* Radiocarbon dating — if suitable organic material exists at all
* Independent review, not only team press releases
* 2027 publication: `Durupinar Formation 2026 Research`

Until then, the honest headline is: **interesting site under serious test, nothing proven.**
