Wudu (ablution) is the purification before prayer. It must be in order with intention (*niyyah*) for Allah.

## Wudu steps

1. **Intention** in heart, say *Bismillah*.
2. **Wash hands** 3 times to wrists.
3. **Rinse mouth** 3 times, **nose** 3 times (sniff and blow).
4. **Wash face** 3 times, ear to ear, hairline to chin.
5. **Wash arms** to elbows 3 times, right then left.
6. **Wipe head** once, wipe ears with wet fingers.
7. **Wash feet** to ankles 3 times, right then left, between toes.

## Dua after wudu

> أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ

*Ashhadu alla ilaha illallah...* — I bear witness none is worthy of worship but Allah, and Muhammad is His servant and messenger.

## Tips

Use little water, ensure water reaches all parts, keep order, renew if broken. Wudu also brings spiritual calm before standing in prayer.
