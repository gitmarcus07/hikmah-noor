Ayatul Kursi is verse 255 of Surah Al-Baqarah, described as the greatest verse of the Quran for its powerful description of Allah.

## Ayatul Kursi in Arabic (first part)

> اللَّهُ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ، لَا تَأْخُذُهُ سِنَةٌ وَلَا نَوْمٌ، لَهُ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الْأَرْضِ

Full verse continues about His Kursi extending over heavens and earth and guarding them without fatigue.

## Transliteration

Allahu la ilaha illa Huwal Hayyul Qayyum. La takhuduhu sinatun wa la nawm. Lahu ma fis-samawati wa ma fil-ard...

## English Meaning

Allah — there is no god but He, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer. Neither drowsiness nor sleep overtakes Him. To Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and earth...

## Benefits

- Protection through the night when recited before sleep.
- Recommended after every fard prayer.
- Affirms tawheed, Allah's life, knowledge and authority.

Learn it in parts, understand the meaning, then recite regularly with reflection.
