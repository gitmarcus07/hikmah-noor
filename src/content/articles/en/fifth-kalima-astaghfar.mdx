The fifth Kalima, *Astaghfar* (seeking forgiveness), leaves no sin unmentioned.

## Fifth Kalima in Arabic

> أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ رَبِّي مِنْ كُلِّ ذَنْبٍ أَذْنَبْتُهُ عَمَدًا أَوْ خَطَأً سِرًّا أَوْ عَلَانِيَةً وَأَتُوبُ إِلَيْهِ مِنَ الذَّنْبِ الَّذِي أَعْلَمُ وَمِنَ الذَّنْبِ الَّذِي لَا أَعْلَمُ إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ عَلَّامُ الْغُيُوبِ وَسَتَّارُ الْعُيُوبِ وَغَفَّارُ الذُّنُوبِ وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ الْعَلِيِّ الْعَظِيمِ

## The four pairs it covers

- **Amadan aw khata'an** — deliberate or mistaken.
- **Sirran aw alaniyah** — secret or open.
- **Alladhi a'lam / la a'lam** — sins you remember and sins you forgot.
- Then three names: **Allamul-ghuyub** (Knower of unseen), **Sattarul-uyub** (Concealer of faults), **Ghaffarudh-dhunub** (Forgiver of sins).

Recite it slowly, meaning each pair — then continue to the [sixth kalima](/kalimas/sixth-kalimah-radd-e-kufr/).
