Pehla Kalima **Tayyab** deen ki bunyaad hai.

## Arabic Mein

> لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ مُحَمَّدٌ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ

## Matlab

Allah ke siwa koi mabood nahi; Muhammad (ﷺ) Allah ke Rasool hain.

Dil se yaqeen, zubaan se iqrar aur amal se saboot — namaz, sachchai aur reham. Bachon ko jaldi yaad karayein, roz dohrayein.
