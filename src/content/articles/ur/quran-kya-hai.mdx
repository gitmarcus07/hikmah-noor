Quran Islam ki muqaddas kitab aur 1.9 billion se zyada Musalmanon ki markazi rehnumai hai. Musalman mante hain ke ye Allah ka kalam hai jo Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) par nazil hua, unhone khud nahi likha.

## Lafz Quran ka matlab kya hai?

Quran Arabic root *qara'a* se hai — parhna ya tilawat karna. Tilawat markazi hai: Musalman paanchon namazon mein parhte hain, *tajweed* ke qawaid seekhte hain, aur kai poora Quran hifz karte hain (*hafiz / hafiza*).

## Quran ki tarteeb kya hai?

- **114 surtain**, chand ayaton se lekar saikron tak.
- **Ayat** — infiradi wahdat.
- **30 pare** — taake Ramadan mein roz ek para parh kar poora Quran mukammal ho.
- Pehli surah: Al-Fatiha. Aakhri: An-Nas.

Ye tarteebi nazool par nahi balki ilahi tarteeb par hai — aqeedah, ibadat, qanoon, qisse aur akhlaq ka majmua.

## Nazool ki mukhtasar tareekh

Nazool ka aaghaz Makkah ke qareeb Ghar-e-Hira mein hua jab Muhammad ki umr 40 thi. 23 salon mein aahista aahista ayaten aain — Makki ayaten iman, Tawhid aur aakhirat par; Madani ayaten muashra, namaz, roza, zakat, khandan aur adal par. Sahaba ne yaad kiya aur likha; Abu Bakr ke daur mein jama hua, Uthman ke daur mein ek mutafiq nuskhe par standardize hua jo aaj duniya bhar mein hai.

## Quran kya sikhata hai?

Ek Allah par iman, namaz, sadqa, roza, sachai, sabr, maafi, adal, waldain, parosiyon aur ghareebon ka khayal, Qayamat ke din jawabdehi, aur Adam, Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa, Maryam aur Isa (peace be upon them) jaise nabiyon ke qisse sabaq ke liye.

## Quran vs Hadith

Quran wahi hai. Hadith Prophet ke aqwal aur aamal ki riwayat hai jo batati hai ke is par kaise chalen. Dono ahem hain, lekin Quran buniyadi kitab hai.
